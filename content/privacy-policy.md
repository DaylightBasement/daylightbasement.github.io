# Privacy Policy  
**Last Updated:** November 5, 2025  

At **Daylight Basement Studio**, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.  

---

## 1. Information We Collect  
We do **not** collect any personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.  

The only information we collect is **non-personal game data**, which may include:  
- Gameplay statistics (e.g., scores, progress, achievements, in-game actions)  
- Basic technical information (e.g., game version, platform, crash reports)  

This information helps us improve gameplay balance, fix bugs, and enhance the overall player experience.  

---

## 2. How We Use Game Data  
We use gameplay and performance data solely for the following purposes:  
- Improving game performance and stability  
- Balancing gameplay features  
- Identifying and fixing bugs or errors  

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.  

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security  
All collected data is stored securely. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.  

---

## 4. Third-Party Services  
Some versions of our games may use third-party platforms (such as Steam, Google Play, or the App Store) that have their own privacy policies. Please review their policies for information on how they handle your data.  

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy  
Our games are intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.  

---

## 6. Changes to This Policy  
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with an updated “Last Updated” date.
